## 🚀 Academic projects

### 🔹 [D-T Fusion Fuel Cycle Dynamics and Preliminary Design](https://github.com/vair01/dt-fusion-fuel-cycle-modeling)
* **Topics:** `Simulink` `MATLAB` `Tritium Modeling` `Vacuum Cryopumping` `Tokamak`
* Dynamic lumped-parameter simulation of a closed tritium fuel cycle for a $500\text{ MW}_{\text{th}}$ compact tokamak. Sized primary cryopumps under LFL flammability limits and determined startup inventory ($1.45\text{ kg}$) and required TBR ($1.09$) for a 2-year doubling time.

### 🔹 [PROCESS Systems Code – TF Coil Temperature Margin Study](https://github.com/vair01/process-fusion-tf-margin)
* **Topics:** `PROCESS` `Python` `Superconducting Magnets` `Systems Engineering` `LCOE`
* Parametric design and economic analysis of a fusion reactor using the UKAEA **PROCESS** systems code. Evaluated electromagnetic/structural trade-offs in $\text{Nb}_3\text{Sn}$ toroidal field coils, conducting 1D and 2D parameter sweeps and comparing geometric vs. LCOE optimization figures of merit.

### 🔹 [Medical Radionuclide Homogeneous Reactor (HASNR)](https://github.com/vair01/bsc-thesis-med-homogeneous-reactor)
* **Topics:** `MATLAB` `Reactor Physics` `ENDF/B-VIII.0` `BSc Thesis` `Mo-99`
* Bachelor's thesis research project: neutronic criticality design and fuel cycle burnup modeling of a LEU-fueled cylindrical aqueous homogeneous reactor for medical molybdenum-99 production.
